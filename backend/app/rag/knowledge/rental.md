## 11-month agreements
Leases of one year or more must be registered under the Registration Act. That is why many agreements are made for 11 months. Some states have their own tenancy laws requiring registration or reporting regardless of duration.

## Tamil Nadu tenancy law
Tamil Nadu's 2017 tenancy law requires tenancy agreements to be in writing and reported to the Rent Authority. It limits the security deposit to 3 months' rent for residential premises and 6 months' rent for non-residential premises. Check the current procedure before signing.

## Stamp paper
The agreement is printed on non-judicial stamp paper; the value depends on the state, rent and deposit.

## Key clauses
Rent and due date, security deposit and refund conditions, duration and renewal, notice period, rent increase, maintenance and utility charges, permitted use, and repairs.
