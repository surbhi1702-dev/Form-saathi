## Who can use a withdrawal slip
A withdrawal slip is normally used by the account holder in person, at the branch where the account is kept, together with the passbook. To give money to someone else, use a cheque instead.

## Filling the slip
Write the account number, name, date and the amount in both figures and words. The figures and words must match exactly. Sign the same way as your specimen signature in the bank's records - a mismatched signature is the most common reason for rejection.

## Amount in words
Write 'Rupees ... Only', for example 'Rupees Five Thousand Only', so nothing can be added later.
