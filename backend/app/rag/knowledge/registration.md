## Executant and claimant
The executant is the person transferring the property (seller or donor). The claimant is the person receiving it (buyer or receiver).

## Stamp duty and registration fee
Stamp duty and registration fee are charged as a percentage of the sale consideration or the government guideline value, whichever is higher. In Tamil Nadu, a sale deed has commonly attracted 7% stamp duty and 2% registration fee, but rates change - always check the current rates on the registration department website. Settlement deeds in favour of family members often have a concessional, capped rate; gift deeds to non-family members are charged like a sale.

## Guideline value
The guideline value is the minimum value fixed by the government for land in each area. It can be looked up online using the village and survey number.

## Encumbrance certificate
An encumbrance certificate (EC) shows registered transactions on the property, such as earlier sales or mortgages. Check it before buying.

## At the Sub-Registrar office
Both parties and two witnesses attend with original ID proofs. Photos and biometrics are taken. High-value transactions require PAN.
