## New assessment
A newly constructed building must be assessed for property tax. The Corporation uses plinth area, usage (residential/commercial), location (street/zone rate) and age of the building to calculate tax.

## Name transfer
After buying, inheriting or receiving a property as a gift, the tax record should be moved to the new owner's name. You need the registered deed and the latest tax receipt.

## Usage and occupancy
Commercial use is taxed at a higher rate than residential. Some cities tax rented-out property differently from owner-occupied property.

## Paying tax
Property tax is usually paid half-yearly. Late payment attracts penalty interest.
