## Nominee
A nominee receives the account balance if the account holder dies. Adding a nominee avoids long legal procedures for the family. If the nominee is a minor, a guardian is also named.

## BSBD accounts
A Basic Savings Bank Deposit (BSBD) account has no minimum balance requirement and comes with basic services like a debit card. Ask for it if you cannot keep a minimum balance.

## Annual income and occupation
Banks ask these for risk categorisation under KYC rules. An approximate range is fine.
