## What you apply for
You can apply for a water supply connection, a sewer connection, or both. The building must be in an area served by Metrowater pipelines.

## Charges
Connection charges depend on building usage (residential, commercial, mixed), number of floors, number of dwelling units and built-up area. Commercial and larger buildings pay more.

## Documents
Property tax receipt (assessment number), ownership proof such as sale deed, approved building plan, ID proof and photo.

## Applying
Apply online on the Metrowater website or at the depot/area office for your locality.
