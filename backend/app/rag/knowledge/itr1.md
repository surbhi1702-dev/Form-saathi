## Who can use ITR-1
ITR-1 is for resident individuals with total income up to Rs. 50 lakh from salary or pension, one house property, other sources such as interest, and agricultural income up to Rs. 5,000. It cannot be used by company directors, people holding unlisted shares, or people with foreign assets or income. Rules change every year, so check the current eligibility on the e-filing portal.

## Old vs new regime
The new tax regime is the default. It has lower slab rates but allows very few deductions. The old regime allows deductions such as Section 80C (PF, PPF, life insurance, ELSS, tuition fees, home loan principal, up to Rs. 1.5 lakh) and Section 80D (health insurance premium). Compare tax under both before choosing.

## Standard deduction
Salaried people get a standard deduction from salary income: Rs. 75,000 in the new regime and Rs. 50,000 in the old regime (for recent years - check the current figure).

## Form 16, 26AS and AIS
Form 16 from your employer shows salary (Part B) and TDS (Part A). Form 26AS and the Annual Information Statement (AIS) on the portal show all TDS and reported income such as bank interest. Your return should match these.

## Verification
After filing, the return must be e-verified (Aadhaar OTP, net banking, or demat) within 30 days, or it is treated as not filed.
