## What PMT-06 is
PMT-06 is the challan used to deposit GST into the electronic cash ledger. You create it on the GST portal under Services > Payments > Create Challan.

## Tax heads
Payments are split into IGST, CGST, SGST/UTGST and Cess. For each head you may pay tax, interest, penalty, fees and other amounts. For sales within the same state, tax is split equally between CGST and SGST. IGST applies to inter-state supplies and imports.

## Late fee
Late fee for delayed returns is levied equally under CGST and SGST.

## QRMP scheme
Taxpayers in the Quarterly Return Monthly Payment scheme pay tax for the first two months of a quarter through PMT-06 using 'Monthly payment for quarterly return'.

## Validity and payment modes
A generated challan (CPIN) is valid for 15 days. Payment can be made by net banking, card, UPI, NEFT/RTGS, or over the counter at authorised banks (over-the-counter has an upper limit per challan).
