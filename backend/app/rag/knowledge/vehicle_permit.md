## Why a permit
A transport vehicle (goods carriage or passenger vehicle on hire) needs a permit from the transport authority to operate commercially.

## Types
A state permit covers operation within a state or region. A national permit lets goods carriages operate across India. An All India Tourist Permit covers tourist vehicles. Temporary permits cover short periods or specific trips.

## Documents
RC, valid insurance, fitness certificate, PUC certificate and road tax receipt. All must be valid when you apply.

## Applying
Apply through the Parivahan permit service or at the RTO. Fees depend on permit type and vehicle category.
