## Domestic and commercial cylinders
Domestic cylinders (14.2 kg and 5 kg) are for household cooking. Commercial 19 kg cylinders are for hotels, restaurants and businesses and are sold at market price.

## One connection per household
Domestic LPG is generally allowed as one connection per household (same kitchen/address). Duplicate connections are detected through Aadhaar and address checks.

## Security deposit
New connections need a refundable security deposit for the cylinder and pressure regulator, plus charges for the stove hose and installation. A double bottle connection (DBC) needs a deposit for the second cylinder.

## Subsidy and Ujjwala
Subsidy, where applicable, is paid directly to the bank account linked with Aadhaar (DBTL/PAHAL). Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana provides deposit-free connections to eligible women from poor households.

## KYC
The distributor collects a KYC form with ID proof, address proof and photo. Applications can also be made on the oil company's website.
