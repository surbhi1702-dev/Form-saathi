## Domestic vs commercial
Domestic tariff applies to homes. Shops, offices, clinics and other businesses come under commercial tariff, which is charged at a higher rate. Using a domestic connection for business can lead to penalties.

## Connected load
Connected load is the total power rating of all appliances, in kilowatts. Add the wattage of each appliance and divide by 1000. Typical ratings: ceiling fan about 75 W, LED light about 15 W, fridge about 200 W, TV about 100 W, 1.5 ton AC about 1500 W, water heater about 2000 W, motor pump about 750 W. Declaring a realistic load avoids penalties and wrong deposit amounts.

## Single phase vs three phase
Single phase supply is enough for most homes. Three phase is needed for heavier loads such as several ACs, large motors or machinery. Boards usually require three phase above a certain load.

## Documents
Ownership proof (sale deed, patta, or property tax receipt), ID proof, and photos. Tenants need the owner's consent letter and a rent agreement. Multi-storey and larger buildings may need a completion certificate or approved plan. Charges and deposits depend on load and category.

## Applying
Apply online through the new-connection portal or at the section office. Note the application/registration number to track status.
