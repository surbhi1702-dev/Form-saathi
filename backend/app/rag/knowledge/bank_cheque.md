## Validity
A cheque is valid for three months from the date written on it. After that it is a 'stale' cheque and banks will not pay it.

## Account payee crossing
Two parallel lines at the top-left corner with 'A/c Payee' written between them mean the money can only be credited to the payee's bank account. This protects you if the cheque is lost.

## Writing safely
Write the payee name exactly as in their bank account and draw a line after it. Write the amount in words starting with 'Rupees' and ending with 'Only'. Write the figures close to the rupee symbol and end with '/-'. Do not overwrite or correct; changes to the date, payee or amount are not accepted on CTS cheques - use a new leaf. Use the same pen for the whole cheque, and sign as per bank records.

## Post-dated cheques
A cheque with a future date can be deposited only on or after that date.
