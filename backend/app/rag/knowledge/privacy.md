## What is stored
Form Saathi keeps your answers only in memory for the length of your session (30 minutes of inactivity by default) and deletes them when you close the session. Aadhaar, PAN and account numbers are masked on screen.

## Aadhaar masking
When giving Aadhaar copies to banks and offices, you can use a masked Aadhaar that shows only the last 4 digits, where the office accepts it.
