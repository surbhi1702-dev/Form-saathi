## What KYC means
KYC (Know Your Customer) is how a bank confirms who you are and where you live. Banks must periodically update KYC (re-KYC). High-risk accounts are updated more often than low-risk ones.

## Officially Valid Documents (OVDs)
Banks accept these as proof of identity and address: passport, driving licence, voter ID card, job card issued by NREGA, proof of possession of Aadhaar number, and a letter from the National Population Register with name and address. Aadhaar is accepted, but the bank should mask all but the last 4 digits on copies it keeps.

## PAN or Form 60
Banks ask for PAN for most accounts. If you don't have a PAN, you fill Form 60, a declaration that you don't have one.

## Re-KYC without changes
If nothing has changed in your details, many banks accept a simple self-declaration through branch, email, net banking or ATM instead of fresh documents. If your address changed, you give the new address proof.

## Tips
Write your name exactly as on the ID you submit. Self-attest copies by signing them. Carry originals for verification.
