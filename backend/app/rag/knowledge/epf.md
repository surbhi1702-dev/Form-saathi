## What Form 11 is
Form 11 is a declaration a new employee gives to the employer. It tells the employer whether you were a PF/EPS member earlier, so your old account can be linked instead of opening a new one.

## UAN
The Universal Account Number (UAN) is a 12-digit number that stays the same across jobs. If you had PF at a previous job, give the same UAN. Having two UANs causes problems when withdrawing or transferring money.

## EPS
The Employees' Pension Scheme is the pension part of PF. Most people who had PF were also EPS members.

## International worker
This is for foreign nationals working in India, or Indians who worked in a country that has a Social Security Agreement with India. Most employees answer No.

## KYC
Link Aadhaar, PAN and bank account to your UAN on the EPFO member portal so claims and transfers work smoothly.
