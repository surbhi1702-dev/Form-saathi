## Post name
Copy the post name and advertisement number exactly as in the notification. Many organisations reject applications with the wrong post code.

## Experience
Count only paid work. Give total years, using decimals for part years. Keep experience and relieving letters ready.

## Notice period
The time you must serve at your current job after resigning, usually 30, 60 or 90 days.
